🃏 How to play

  1. You see cards with ? on them. They hide pairs.
  2. A pair is a fact and its answer.
    Example: 3 × 4 and 12.
  3. Tap one card. Then tap a second card.
  4. Same pair? The cards turn green and stay open. 🎉
  5. Not a pair? The cards turn back. Try to remember them!
  6. Find all pairs to win. Fewer tries means more stars: ⭐⭐⭐ for 9 tries or fewer.
